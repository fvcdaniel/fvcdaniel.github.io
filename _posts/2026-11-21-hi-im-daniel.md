Hi! I'm Daniel Cardoso, a DevOps Engineer based in João Pessoa, Paraíba, Brazil. This is the first post on the blog, so it seems only fair to start by introducing myself.

## Background

I've been working in tech since 2009. I started as a trainee at UFPB, working with Ruby, Rails and Java, while studying Internet Systems Technology at IFPB (2007 to 2010).

After that I worked at Lojas Maia/Magazine Luiza as a systems analyst (2010 to 2013), with Java, Oracle and PL/SQL. Then I moved to CODATA as an information security analyst (2013 to 2016), where I worked with Linux, iptables, JBoss, Tomcat, Docker, Kubernetes, Rancher and vCenter.

In 2016 I went back to IFPB, this time as a systems analyst, and stayed until 2021. At the same institute I was also a substitute professor (2015 to 2017) and network and infrastructure coordinator (2019 to 2022). It was a period full of datacenter infrastructure: vCenter, FortiGate, Linux, Kubernetes with Rancher, GitLab, Graylog, Terraform and Ansible.

In December 2021 I joined Oddie as a DevOps Engineer, working remotely until July 2023. For the first few months I balanced Oddie with the network and infrastructure coordination at IFPB, which I kept until May 2022. That was a very cloud-heavy phase: AWS (EC2, SQS, S3, RDS, API Gateway, ELB, Lambda), GCP, Terraform, GitHub Actions, Kubernetes, Kustomize, Keycloak (SSO, OIDC, SAML) and ELK. Between July and September 2023 I was back at IFPB as a systems analyst.

## Today

Since October 2023 I've been a DevOps Engineer at WEX. Day to day I work with AWS, Docker, Kubernetes, Helm, ArgoCD, Terraform and Terragrunt, GitHub Actions, Vault, Artifactory, Lambda and S3, plus observability with Datadog and Grafana.

## What I work with

- Cloud: AWS and GCP
- Containers and orchestration: Docker, Kubernetes, Helm, Rancher and ArgoCD
- Infrastructure as Code: Terraform and Terragrunt
- CI/CD: GitHub Actions and GitLab
- Observability: Datadog, Grafana, Graylog and ELK
- On-premises infrastructure: vCenter, FortiGate and Linux

## What's next

I plan to use this space to write about what I do every day: Kubernetes, GitOps with ArgoCD, Terraform and Terragrunt, CI/CD pipelines, observability, and the experience of moving between on-premises datacenters and the cloud. I'll focus on practical notes, the kind I wish I had found while I was solving the problem.
